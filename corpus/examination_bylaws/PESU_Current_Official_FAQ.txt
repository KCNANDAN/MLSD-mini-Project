QUESTION: What is the grading system followed at PES University?
ANSWER: PES University follows a 10 point grading system incorporating relative and absolute grading.

QUESTION: What is the level of attendance required to be eligible to take the university exams?
ANSWER: 80% attendance is mandatory for a student to take the exam.
