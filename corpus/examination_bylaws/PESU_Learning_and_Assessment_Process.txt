Assessments
Computer Based Tests
e
In-semester assessments (ISAs/Internal) are conducted
online, using our custom built fully automated assessment
platform. The test, evaluation, re-evaluation and feedback is
conducted online in a paperless process. Students and
parents get immediate notification of results.
Pen and Paper mode Assessments
e The end-semester assessment is conducted in pen & paper
mode. The answers scripts are processed using an image
processing algorithm and grouped question-wise. To
eliminate bias each answer is evaluated online without
student details.
